      Need help?
      How do I order?

      Tap + to add an item, and tap it again for more. Tap − to remove one. Then choose delivery or pickup and tap the order button. Your order opens in WhatsApp, ready to send.

      Is the delivery fee in the total?

      No. Delivery fees vary by location and are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

      How do I pick up my order?

      In the Delivery section, tap the switch next to Deliver to me. The label changes to Pickup instead and the pickup location appears. Tap the location to open directions in Google Maps. We'll confirm the pickup time on WhatsApp.

      Can I ask for changes to my fish?

      Yes. Add it in the Note field, for example "less spicy" or "no onions".

      Why can't I add an item?

      Items marked Sold out are finished for now. They become available again when we update the menu.

      Does this page save my details?

      Your name and address are remembered on this phone only, so repeat orders are quicker.
